About Cync Digital

A lean, founder-led practice — not a bigger org chart.

Direct access to the person doing the work, on every engagement. No account handoffs.

Cync Digital is led by Naimur Rahman Ferdous, who brings over 3 years of hands-on experience building and managing performance-based partner networks from zero — negotiating commission structures, owning go-to-market execution, and running partner and affiliate programs across fintech, consumer, and trade & distribution channels.

That track record includes scaling an international fintech partner network to 240+ active acquisition partners with 20%+ month-over-month conversion growth, building an affiliate program from scratch to 100+ partners across creator and publisher channels, and driving partnership performance across a 50+ partner trade and distribution network in a full-time business development role.

We started Cync Digital to bring that same hands-on, accountable approach to more teams — without the overhead of a large agency. When you work with us, you work directly with the person who has actually run the programs, not an account manager relaying to someone else. See Work for the detail behind every figure above.
