DEAL-0138 · FINTECH AFFILIATE PARTNER (NDA)

Hybrid restructure closes a stalled crypto/trading creator

● CLOSED — 9 DAYS
Context

Mid-tier crypto/trading content creator, 60K+ combined following. High-volume but historically low-converting traffic. In talks with two competing platforms.

Objection

Wary of flat CPA (traffic-quality penalties) and wary of pure RevShare (no downside protection if early conversion was slow).

Negotiation Lever

Rebuilt as hybrid: reduced upfront CPA plus a RevShare kicker stepping up past a 90-day active-trader threshold, paired with the Day 1/3/7 activation sequence.

Outcome

Signed within 9 days vs. a 3+ week stall. Structure was later adopted as the standard hybrid tier for new partners.
