How We Work

A clear process, start to finish.

Four phases — so you always know what stage you're in and what happens next.

Phase 01

Audit & Foundation

We pull existing partner and performance data, segment what's actually working, and map every commission model against compliance. You can't scale what hasn't been measured first.

Phase 02

Strategy & Build

We design the commission structure, activation sequence, and recruitment targeting — then build the tracking and automation to run it.

Phase 03

Launch & Activate

We recruit with intent — targeting the partners most likely to convert, not the biggest follower counts — and run the onboarding sequence that gets them active fast.

Phase 04

Optimize & Report

We A/B test, tighten what's underperforming, and hand over a full results report against baseline — with the tracker and SOPs so the program keeps running without us.

Every engagement is scoped to your program's size and goals. Tell us where you're starting from on the Contact page and we'll give you a straight answer on fit and cost before we go further.
